After the violan collapse imperial cohesion broke down completely in the region of Gellerthal. In the already highly fragmented area in which pretty much almost every single major settlement is its own imperial prince this acted as fuel to a belligerent fire. Now the feuds between those princes flared up even harder in complete opposition to the [[Prima Bulla Imperialis]]. [[Veshni]] having become fully corrupt and complacent since the [[Guilder War]] ended done little to uphold peace and even the church does not manage to keep it outside its few theocratic territories in the area. Now the highly fragmented [[Human]], [[Elf|elven]], [[Gnome]], [[Dwarf]], and [[Halfling]] realms of the area fight for control over the fertile plains with no clear victor ever emerging.
